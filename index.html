ABOUT ME

AEROSPACE & ROCKETRY STUDENT, DARTMOUTH COLLEGE

Hello! I am an aspiring aerospace engineer with a deep passion for space exploration and high-powered rocketry. I founded India's first high school rocketry team to successfully achieve a 1-kilometer altitude launch, and I currently build with Dartmouth Student Rocketry in Hanover, NH. I specialize in hands-on engineering—designing and building systems that range from high-altitude rockets to AI-powered wildlife catalogers.
